About Me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer with 6+ years of experience building high-performance backend systems for high-scale, mission-critical products in the financial, payments, retail and iGaming sectors.

I specialize in Java, Spring Boot, microservices and event-driven architecture. At PicPay, I led the DDA (Direct Debit Authorization) front, reducing notification time from 2m40s to under 15 seconds and cutting operating costs; I also delivered query performance gains from 3 minutes to under 1 second and from 15 seconds to 200 ms.

I work end-to-end, from development to infrastructure (AWS, Kafka, EKS), with a strong culture of testing, code review and observability.

I'm passionate about sharing knowledge with the developer community through Rust4Noobs, a beginner-friendly Rust guide created for the he4rt community. Currently improving my AI skills and exploring new technologies.
